The short version

  • BlockBop does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app works fully offline. Everything you create stays on your device.
  • The camera is optional and photos never leave the device.
  • Settings for grown-ups and any link that leaves the app are protected by a grown-up check.

1. Who we are

BlockBop: Block Builder 3D (“BlockBop”, “the app”) is made by Bombay Candy Designs (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the BlockBop app on Android.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. BlockBop has no user accounts and no servers that receive information from the app. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise or approximate location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage analytics or crash reports.

3. Information that stays on your device

To make the app work, BlockBop saves some things in its own private storage on your device. This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it.

WhatWhy
Builder name (optional)To greet the player on the home screen. It can be any nickname.
Progress: finished toys, badges, levels, chosen avatar and bannerTo remember progress and rewards between sessions.
My bricks: brick boxes and countsTo show which toys can be built with the bricks you have.
Build photos and scanned brick photos (optional)To show your own pictures of finished builds and of scanned bricks.
Free buildsTo let you continue and keep your own creations.
Settings (sound, appearance)To remember your preferences.

You can delete all of this at any time with Settings → For grown-ups & parents → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

  • Camera (optional). Used only when you tap “Take a photo” to save a picture of your build or to scan your bricks. Photos are processed on the device to find bricks and are never uploaded. If you deny the camera permission, all other features keep working, and you can choose a photo from your gallery instead.
  • Internet. The app includes Android’s standard internet permission, which is part of the framework the app is built with. BlockBop does not send any information over the internet. Links to our email address or website open only after a grown-up check, in your own mail app or browser.

5. Read-aloud voice

Build steps can be read aloud using your device’s built-in text-to-speech voice. BlockBop does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

6. Children’s privacy

BlockBop is designed for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, there is no personal information for us to use, share, sell or disclose. We do not show advertising and we do not include any third-party advertising or analytics code.

We designed BlockBop with children’s privacy laws and Google Play’s Families policies in mind, including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Grown-up check

Before unlocking all levels, resetting progress, sharing the app, rating it, or opening any link outside the app, BlockBop asks a short question that a grown-up answers. This helps keep children inside the app and stops accidental changes.

8. Third parties

BlockBop does not include third-party advertising, analytics, social media or tracking services. The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download, install or update apps, as described in Google’s own privacy policy.

9. Data security and retention

Information stays in the app’s private storage on your device, protected by your device’s security. We keep nothing on our side, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Information on the device remains until you reset progress or uninstall the app.

10. Your choices and rights

You are always in control: you can change or remove the builder name, delete photos, reset all progress, or uninstall the app. Because we do not hold any of your information, we have nothing to provide, correct or delete on request. If you have any questions about this, please contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how BlockBop handles information, for example by adding an optional backup feature, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change, and we will ask for permission where required before anything is collected.
